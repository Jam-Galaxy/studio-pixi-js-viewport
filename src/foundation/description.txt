Domain-agnostic functions and modules with pure functions and primitives (geometry, algebra, collections, Units, secure utilities) are located here. The domain layer MAY depend on them. Modules located here should be considered a language extension. This layer should NOT know anything about the domain layer.
This is NOT infrastructure. There should be no I/O, databases, files, HTTP, clocks, randomness, or other external details inside.
These are shared, pure libraries (the technical core).
